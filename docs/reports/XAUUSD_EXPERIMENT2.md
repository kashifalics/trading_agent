## Execution and cost assumptions (identical for setups and controls)

* **Entry:** a limit order placed at the close of the signal candle, live for 20 candles, cancelled if a candle closes beyond the far edge first.
* **Conservative fill:** the ask (longs) or bid (shorts) must trade beyond the limit by the spread measured at the signal candle; the fill minute is found on 1-minute data. In the fill minute only adverse prices count.
* **Stop:** far edge + 0.1 ATR, at least 0.25 ATR from the fill. Exits at the bid (longs) or ask (shorts); the stop candle is replayed minute by minute; a stop minute that opens beyond the stop fills at that open.
* **Targets and holds:** 1.5R, 2R, 3R (main 2R); time stop H4 60 candles, H8 30 candles, D1 20 candles; other holds are description only.
* **Gaps:** a trade is excluded only if an unscheduled gap (30+ minutes, not a US holiday) starts while it is actually open.
* **Financing:** nights held (17:00 New York rollovers, Friday counting 3) times the scenario cost. **Main scenario:** fixed $0.10 per ounce per night. Also reported: no financing; and a FRED-based proxy (fed funds + 2.5% for longs; 2.5% − fed funds for shorts). The proxy is **not** a historical broker swap rate.

The proxy scenario is reported as unavailable, never estimated..
* **Spread:** Dukascopy's own bid/ask on every trade; stress tests add 1.5× spread or $0.10 slippage per side.
