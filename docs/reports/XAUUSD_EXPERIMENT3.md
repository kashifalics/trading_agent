## Fixes and definitions

* F1 daily-break gaps are scheduled closures; F2 levels refreshed every trading week; F3 the band nearest the breakout close;
  F4 gap list limited to the development period; F5 the wider definitions below.

| Setup | Definition |
|---|---|
| E1_order_block_after_break | order block whose impulse broke structure (BOS or CHoCH), displacement >= 1.5 ATR |
| E2_fvg_displacement_1p5 | fair value gap whose middle candle has range >= 1.5 ATR and a body >= 70% of its range |
| E3_broken_level_retest | support/resistance band (levels refreshed weekly) broken by a close >= 0.25 ATR beyond it; limit at the broken edge |
| E4_zone_pullback_d1_trend | demand (supply) zone with the D1 structure trend in the trade's direction |
| E5_prev_day_sweep_reclaim | price trades beyond the previous New York day's low (high) and a candle of the same day closes back inside |

## Execution and costs (identical for setups and controls)

* Limit order at the signal close, live 20 candles; conservative fill (trade through by the signal-time spread); stop = far edge + 0.1 ATR; main target 2R; time stop H1 48 candles, H4 60 candles.
* Real Dukascopy bid/ask; financing $0.10 per ounce per night (main), also no financing and the FRED proxy when available (FRED data: NOT available).
* Long and short are pooled per test; every trade is compared with random limit orders of the same direction, distance, width, session, volatility and year.

## Limitations

* One data source (Dukascopy); a broker's spreads, stop rules, limit-order queue and swaps differ.
* Development results are hypotheses: experiment 3 was designed after experiments 1-2 and the diagnostic. Only the sealed test (with the owner's separate approval) or forward paper trading can confirm a candidate.
* The power gate counts fills at candle level (the simulator can still reject a few at minute level).
