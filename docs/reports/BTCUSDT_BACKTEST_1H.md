## Strategy and execution assumptions

* Strategy: `decision_engine` — the production BUY/SELL/WAIT policy restricted to price-action evidence of this single timeframe (no analogues, ML or multi-timeframe). **Parameters are the library defaults; nothing was tuned on this data.**
* A signal is generated from bars up to and including bar *i*; the order fills at the **open of bar i+1** (never the signal close). Evaluated on every bar while flat (`eval_every=1`).
* The plan's *entry zone* is not used: entries are market orders at the next open. Stop and first target come from the plan.
* If one bar contains both stop and target, **the stop is assumed hit first**. Gaps through a stop fill at the open.
* Sizing: 1% of current equity risked per trade (stop distance), leverage capped at 20×, one position at a time, forced exit after 200 bars or at end of data.
* The strategy's internal data-quality gate is held at ≥ 0.9 (history is complete by construction here).
* Costs are percentages of price so they stay realistic across a 16k → 120k price history:

| Scenario | Fee / side | Spread (full) | Slippage / market fill | Round-trip total |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `zero_cost` | 0% | 0% | 0% | 0.000% |
| `optimistic` | 0.075% | 0.001% | 0.005% | 0.161% |
| `base` | 0.1% | 0.002% | 0.01% | 0.222% |
| `stress` | 0.1% | 0.01% | 0.03% | 0.270% |

Binance spot's standard taker fee is 0.10% per side (0.075% with BNB discount); BTCUSDT's quoted spread is typically a tick or two, so the spread input is deliberately conservative. **`base` is the headline scenario.**

## Limitations

* One instrument and one historical path; folds are serially dependent and the sample covers a limited set of regimes.
* No holiday/maintenance modelling is needed for 24/7 BTCUSDT, but exchange outages and order-book depth are not modelled.
* Results are for fixed default rules. Any later parameter change must be validated on a period not used here.
